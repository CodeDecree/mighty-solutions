Traffic Safety

Toll Plaza Fog Light

LED-based fog warning lights designed specifically for toll plazas and highway entry points. These high-intensity amber and white LED lights are installed on crash barriers or bull nose positions to provide clear visibility to motorists during low-visibility conditions such as fog, heavy rain, or nighttime operations.

  • 300mm/200mm diameter LED-based signal aspects
  • Synchronized flashing mode (1 sec ON, 1 sec OFF)
  • Weather-resistant polycarbonate or mild steel housing
  • Low power consumption (less than 8W)
  • Visibility range up to 500 meters

Lane Control

Over Head Lane Signal Light (OHLS)

Overhead lane control signals mounted on toll plaza canopies to guide traffic through designated lanes. These signals feature LED-based red cross and green arrow indicators in a single compact module, providing clear lane instructions to motorists approaching toll booths.

  • Red Cross and Green Arrow in single 2-in-1 module
  • Available in 200mm, 300mm, and 425mm sizes
  • Canopy-mounted design for optimal visibility
  • Suitable for toll plazas and parking management
  • UV-stabilized polycarbonate or steel housing

Eco-Friendly

Solar Blinker and Solar Sign Board

Solar-powered LED blinker units and illuminated sign boards that operate autonomously without external electricity supply. These eco-friendly warning systems are ideal for highways, toll plazas, and construction zones, featuring automatic day-night operation with integrated charge controllers and batteries.

  • 100% solar-powered with 12V/24V battery backup
  • Available in red, amber, and green colors
  • Blinking cycle: 1 second ON, 1 second OFF

  • Zero electricity cost with 5+ years lifespan
  • IP65 weatherproof rating
