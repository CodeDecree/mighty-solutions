Driver Awareness

Traffic Signal Countdown Timer

Digital countdown timer displays that show the remaining time for current traffic signal indications (red, green, yellow). These intelligent devices improve driver decision-making at intersections by eliminating the dilemma zone, allowing drivers to know exactly when signals will change. Studies show countdown timers reduce start-up time losses by an average of 0.82 seconds, significantly improving intersection efficiency.

  • Available in 6", 8", 12", and 15" digit sizes
  • Dual color display: Red and Green LED segments
  • Count range: 00-99 (double digit) or 000-999 (triple digit)
  • Visibility range up to 400 meters
  • Operating voltage: 220V/110V AC or 12V/24V DC
  • Temperature range: -20°C to +60°C
  • UV-stabilized polycarbonate housing with IP65 rating
  • Auto-sensing with 2-3 cycle validation
